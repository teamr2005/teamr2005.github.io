Lastswipe Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-05 · Developer: Woojin Kang, Ontario, Canada · Contact: teamr321@gmail.com

Summary

Lastswipe works on your phone. It has no accounts and no analytics. The information it uses to block short videos never leaves your device.

1. Accessibility Service

Lastswipe uses Android's Accessibility Service API so it can block short-video feeds and apps according to rules you set. With your permission, it accesses:

  • the package name of the app that is currently on screen;
  • in apps where you turn on short-video blocking, the internal names and accessibility labels of interface elements (for example, a view identifying a short-video player, or a selected "Reels" tab);
  • only if you turn on "skip reels in the feed" for an app: when you scroll that app's feed, and where its posts sit on the screen.

It does not read messages, comments, captions or text you type. This information is processed instantly in your phone's memory to decide whether a rule applies. When one does, Lastswipe presses Back or shows a pause screen. If you turned on "skip reels in the feed" for an app, Lastswipe also scrolls that app's feed past short videos for you once you stop scrolling. These are the only actions it takes. Accessibility data is never stored, sent off your device, sold, shared, or used for advertising, analytics or profiling.

2. Information stored on your device

Lastswipe stores, in a private database on your phone only: your rules, schedules and focus profiles; a log of blocked attempts and overrides (app package name, reason, time); minutes spent each day in supported short-video apps; focus-session history; and app settings. History older than about 13 months is deleted automatically. Android cloud backup and device transfer are switched off for this data. You can delete statistics at any time in Settings > Delete all statistics, and everything by uninstalling.

3. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Lastswipe receives purchase status and tokens from Google Play to unlock Lastswipe Plus; it never receives your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Play.

4. Advertising

Free users may see one banner ad on the main screen, served by Google AdMob. Before ads are requested, Lastswipe asks for consent where the law requires it, using Google's User Messaging Platform. AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, as described in Google's documentation. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and change consent in Lastswipe > Settings > Ad privacy choices. Ads are never shown on the pause screen, during focus sessions, or to Plus subscribers.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Lastswipe shows focus-session countdowns and completions. No notification content is sent anywhere.

6. Children

Lastswipe is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Security

Data stays in app-private storage protected by Android's sandbox. Lastswipe does not transmit accessibility data, so there is nothing of it to intercept.

8. Your choices

Turn the Accessibility service off at any time in Android Settings > Accessibility. Delete statistics in the app. Uninstalling removes all Lastswipe data from the device.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date, and material changes will be shown in the app.
