Lastswipe

Block the feed. Keep the app.

Lastswipe pauses YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels without deleting the apps you actually need. Watch a tutorial without falling into Shorts. Reply to a friend without getting pulled into Reels.

Coming soon to Google Play

What it does

  • Short videos only. Pauses YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels (beta), Facebook Reels and Snapchat Spotlight (experimental), while the rest of each app keeps working.
  • Whole apps. Blocks TikTok and other short-video apps entirely.
  • Schedules and daily limits. On workdays, in the evening, at weekends, or once your time for the day is used up.
  • Focus sessions. 25, 45 or 60 minutes with a countdown.
  • A calm pause screen instead of an endless feed. Overrides take a deliberate wait and are counted.

Private by design

No account and no analytics. Your rules and statistics stay on your phone.

Lastswipe uses Android's Accessibility Service to see which app is open and to recognise short-video screens. It only presses Back, shows its pause screen, or, if you turn it on, scrolls a feed past short videos once you stop scrolling. Accessibility data never leaves your phone.

The free version shows one banner ad from Google AdMob on its Today screen, never on the pause screen or during focus. Lastswipe Plus has no ads. Details are in the privacy policy.

Support

Questions, a bug, or blocking that stopped working after an app update? Email teamr321@gmail.com.

To turn Lastswipe off, open Android Settings > Accessibility. Uninstalling it removes all of its data.
